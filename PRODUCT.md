# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One user: Alessio, at the gym, often early in the morning before work. Phone in one hand
between sets, glancing for a few seconds, correcting a number with the thumb. Trains a
Push / Pull / Gambe split, three days in rotation.

## Product Purpose

Replace the iPhone note where weights were rewritten by hand. Open the day, find the
exercises pre-filled with last time's values, correct what changed, tick the set, rest,
repeat. Success: logging a set takes one tap in the common case, and progress over weeks
is visible without effort.

## Brand Personality

Clean and calm, in the family of Apple Fitness and Salute: generous spacing, soft
surfaces, big rounded numerals. One color per training day (corallo Push, blu Pull,
verde Gambe) so the day is recognizable at a glance.

## Anti-references

Loud "gym bro" aesthetics: neon green on black, aggressive condensed type, motivational
slogans. Dense spreadsheet-like loggers. Gamification noise (streak flames, badges).

## Design Principles

1. The workout screen is the product: every other screen is secondary.
2. Pre-fill everything; the user only corrects.
3. Numbers are the hero: large, rounded, tabular.
4. Color identifies the day, never decorates.
5. Nothing is lost: every change is saved immediately, offline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in dark and light, tap targets at least 44px (inputs 50px), body text at
least 16px, visible focus, labelled inputs for screen readers, reduced motion respected.
